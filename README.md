# Cosimo Curri

## About Me

I hold a Master's Degree in Computer Engineering, specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, from [Politecnico di Bari](https://www.poliba.it/en/laurea-magistrale-ingegneria-informatica), with an academic and research background in machine learning, deep learning and software engineering.

My work combines data-driven experimentation, model development and evaluation, and the design of AI-enabled software systems, with particular emphasis on reproducibility, analytical rigor and technical documentation.

## Research Experience

**Research Intern — [Politecnico di Bari](https://www.poliba.it/en/laurea-magistrale-ingegneria-informatica)**  
May 2025 – Sep 2026

Conducted academic research on graph-based recommender systems, focusing on Deep Learning, Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), and top-K recommendation. Reproduced and benchmarked recommendation models across multiple datasets through controlled experiments. Investigated the impact of graph sampling, architectural configurations, and training strategies on recommendation performance. Evaluated experimental results using ranking metrics such as Recall@K and nDCG@K, emphasizing reproducibility and critical interpretation. Organized experimental code, configurations, execution scripts, and documentation to support reproducible research, and contributed to the preparation of a scientific manuscript.

## Project Portfolio

Individual and collaborative academic projects, grouped by their primary technical focus. Each repository includes documentation, setup instructions and contributor credits.

### Machine Learning & Data Science

- **[Low-Light Image Enhancement](https://github.com/cosimo-curri/low-light-enhancement-cross-dataset)** — Individual project comparing four deep learning model families through validation-based hyperparameter selection, multi-seed evaluation and cross-dataset testing, with 624 archived run logs.
